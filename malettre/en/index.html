Counting the days, my sweet

Happy Valentine's Day, my sweet. I think of our first date by the sea in Lomé. The storm came out of nowhere, and we ran to hide under a coconut seller's straw hut. I remember laughing while the rain drummed on the straw, and thinking: I want more days like this one.

What caught me first was your smile, and then your voice, which calms me even when I did not know I was worried. You are gentle and you are patient, and that is rare. You make room for people without making any noise about it.

Now I am counting the days until you are back from Accra. The house is quieter without you. Come home soon, and I will be waiting, with the storm or without it.

Questions

What people
ask us.

How does the person receive the letter?

Through a link you send them yourself, on WhatsApp or anywhere else, whenever you like: three days before, or the night before. We never write to the person. They open the link on their phone, with nothing to install, and find a sealed envelope waiting for its hour.

What do they see if they open the link before the time?

A closed envelope and a countdown, nothing else: the seal sleeps and no button opens it. At the chosen time, the wax lights up and the button appears, without reloading the page. Their phone's clock is what counts: midnight in Abidjan is not midnight in Cotonou.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The letter is a page that opens in the phone's browser, even on a weak connection. There is no app to download and no account to create, neither for you nor for the person who receives it.

How much does a letter cost?

It is free to start: online for 7 days, one photo, our signature at the bottom. Paid offers keep it for a year, without our signature, with your choice of finish: The Note (2 000 F), The Letter (5 000 F) and The Keepsake (10 000 F). The Quill adds 500 F, only if you send the version it wrote.

How do I pay?

By Mobile Money or card, on our payment provider's secure page, where you choose your network. Nothing to copy, no code to give us. A free letter never goes through checkout at all: you write it, you get your link.

What is the Quill?

A writing aid. You answer a few questions, share a memory, say how you want your letter, and the Quill writes a first version. You reread it, change what you like, and sign. Trying it costs nothing, and it never makes up a memory for you.

Is my letter private?

Its address is drawn at random, never indexed by search engines, and it expires: seven days for a free letter, a year for a paid one. But anyone with the link can read it: it is a wrapped gift, not a safe. And if the person who receives it asks for it to be removed, it is removed within 24 hours.

Can you make our wedding invitation?

Yes, on request, for 25 000 F, made to measure by our team. It opens like a letter, with the seal bearing your initials, the countdown to the big day, the schedule, the venue, the dress code and your photos. Your guests reply straight to your WhatsApp.
